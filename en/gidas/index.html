The forest whispers to you…

Whispering Alley

Thousands of glowing mushrooms show you the way to memories, feelings and secrets — they are already whispering something to you… Can you hear them? Or is it something else whispering? Stay on the path: Whispering Alley leads you in the right direction. Listen for magic, be brave around the forest creatures and let yourself drift into the memories shimmering in the depths of the pond.

Pond of Memories

Sometimes memories hide very deep — as if underwater, as if in a dream, even though they are still somewhere in your mind. That is why we meet by the Pond of Memories, whose reflections bring you back to things you may have forgotten. Frightening or joyful, every memory has a place at the bottom of the pond. So ask your questions boldly!

Slumbering Rose Garden

Although roses fall asleep in autumn, those in our Rose Garden are only dozing. And these are no ordinary earthly roses. Rumour has it that the Magical Roses came from space, where a little fellow grew them on asteroid B-612. Discover their thoughtful nature, and do not be afraid to pause and think for yourself.

Herbarium

We are in the Forest Garden, so explore its Herbarium, home to extraordinary botanical beings — and not all of them are plants. Look closely: each has a character of its own. They do more than grow. They glow, give off scents, touch, listen and perhaps even taste you. Be like them: curious and true to yourself.

Grove of Secrets

If all these impressions have made your head spin, share a sigh with the Stumps and wander into the Grove of Secrets, tucked into a corner of the Garden. The forest will share its secrets and gladly listen to yours. Everyone has secrets — soft ones, sharp-edged ones and ones with smooth little thorns. Sometimes you long to share them… when it feels safe. The forest hopes you feel safe here.

Forest Photography Vaults

quiet forest discoveries by photographer Urtė Statkutė

Just look at these finds! Not edible boletes or chanterelles, but tiny, sometimes barely noticeable forest dwellers that you need to seek out, look at closely and understand!

If you love the forest, we are heading the same way. For me, the forest is a kind of shelter. I simply spend time there; sometimes I cry, sometimes I meditate. In the forest I feel small, in the best possible sense: if I am small in such a vast world, then my problems are small too. What do you think of that?

One day, among the tall grass, I spotted a tiny inedible mushroom. I photographed it, and a new path seemed to open up. That little mushroom became the first subject of “Urtės miškografija” — Urtė’s forest photography — which you can now explore too.

Tiny mushrooms also fascinate me because their world only reveals itself when we slow down.

Today I would like to show you the treasures I have discovered and captured with my camera. I wonder if you will recognise the rare, protected bolete known in Lithuanian as dėmėtasis perkūnbaravykis?

Look more closely at the photographs and you will see what happens when a mushroom is taken over by other fungi — mould, to be precise. Isn’t it beautiful?

From the bottom of my heart, I hope you enjoy the mushrooms, lichens and slime moulds, in all their shapes and sizes.
